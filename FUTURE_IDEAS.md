# Future UI / UX Ideas

Ideas for enhancing the 8x8x8 Sudoku solver experience in future iterations:

1. **Auto-Remove Candidates**:
   - Automatically remove matching candidate marks from cells in the same row, column, shaft, or 2x2x2 region when a digit is entered.
2. **Digit Highlighting**:
   - Clicking or hovering a digit (or pressing a modifier + 1–8) highlights all occurrences of that digit across the entire 8x8x8 cube.
4. **Multi-Step Undo / Redo History**:
   - Expand from 1-layer undo/redo to a full multi-step history stack (`Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y`).
5. **Slice Isolation / Depth Fog / Opacity Slider**:
   - Slider or toggle to hide or further fade cells outside the active plane, or show only the active 2-layer 2x2x2 slab.
6. **Quick Camera Presets**:
   - Buttons or hotkeys (`1`–`6` numpad or `X`/`Y`/`Z` view buttons) to snap the camera to face XY, XZ, or YZ planes cleanly, plus a "Reset Camera" button.
7. **Puzzle Browser & Progress Tracker**:
   - A grid/list modal showing puzzle numbers 1..100 with solved checkmarks, completion timestamps, and elapsed solve time.
8. **Solve Timer**:
   - Solve timer (pauses when tab is hidden).
9. **Hint / Check Cell**:
   - "Check" button to verify current entries against the unique solution, or "Hint" to reveal one cell.
10. **Two-Finger Pinch Zoom**:
    - Pinch-to-zoom camera distance support on touch devices.
